# Product And Writing Skill Decisions

Status: accepted for the September 2026 product and writing update.

## Owner Mapping

The research from Marty Cagan's *Inspired* and *Пиши, сокращай* extends existing
owners. A book or a chapter is not itself a reason to create another skill.

| Material | Existing owner | Added or refined guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Inspired | `product-design` | Shared discovery, early engineering input, roles and authority, prototype/evidence selection, and the difference between learning and release readiness |
| Inspired | `business-product-consulting` | Product strategy, customer partners, business constraints, leadership capability, and scoped operating-model interventions |
| Пиши, сокращай | `information-writing` | Supported informative detail, structure and navigation, and professional genres |
| Пиши, сокращай | `information-editing` | Revision choices, conditional editing order, and meaning-preserving examples |
| Пиши, сокращай | `audience-adaptation` | Reader preparation, consumption mode, and distinct reader questions |
| Пиши, сокращай | `information-explanation` | Concrete examples and practical explanatory depth |
| Пиши, сокращай | `technical-writing` | Preparation, sequencing, and returning midway through procedures |
| Пиши, сокращай | `information-presentation` | Live versus independent reading, visible qualifications, and informative visual evidence |

These changes add nine focused reference files and extend relevant existing
references. They do not add a separate product-management skill or a book-named
writing skill. The expanded owners remain in their existing installation bundles.

## Boundaries And Installation

Product guidance must not reopen settled choices or require discovery ceremonies
for bounded work. Writing guidance must not turn concise prose into lost evidence,
missing conditions, or a compulsory output template.

The public repository contains reusable guidance, not book files, research chunks,
agent transcripts, or temporary prompts. Installation remains flat under
`skills/<skill-name>`; references are loaded only when relevant.

Use the [catalog](../CATALOG.md) for ownership and bundle selection and the
[installation guide](../README.md#install-skills) for fresh installs and updates.
