# Professional genres

Read only the section for the requested artifact: applications and profiles, company descriptions, first contact, press releases, or product pages. These are content choices under the ordinary writing contract. Technical documents, coordination artifacts, and slide narratives retain their specialized owners. Drafting does not authorize sending or publishing and does not establish business effectiveness.

## Applications and profiles

For a named vacancy, treat its requirements as the reader's questions. Identify the role early and match relevant requirements to accurate capability and inspectable evidence. An annotated portfolio link should say what to inspect and what the applicant actually contributed. A team result does not establish sole authorship.

Hypothetical supplied facts: the applicant designed a booking project's cancellation flow and tested its prototype with the support team. Replace “Experienced designer. Portfolio: [link]” with “Booking project: I designed the cancellation flow and tested its prototype with the support team. Portfolio: [link].” The extra detail comes from the supplied facts, not the original sentence. Distinguish another person's contribution when it resolves a likely ambiguity; do not append disclaimers to every link.

Preserve a relevant mismatch. A truthful learning plan can accompany it, but cannot replace an absent qualification with a claimed one. Without a vacancy, establish a recognizable professional category, then answer the practical questions that determine fit: work offered, intended clients, deliverables, collaboration, and contact. Use only relevant questions. Creative applications and expressive profiles may legitimately prioritize identity, voice, or unusual form.

## Company descriptions and values

Connect a value to an observable choice, action, or deliberate refusal. Connect a credential or operating feature to what it enables for this reader. If the connecting fact is absent, identify it; expanding an acronym or adding praise does not fill the gap.

Hypothetical supplied policy: estimates separate agreed work from optional additions, whose prices are confirmed before work on them begins. Instead of “We believe in transparent collaboration,” describe that process. It is a stronger commitment than the slogan and is appropriate only when the policy is true.

State a real delivery constraint directly, without reprimanding a new reader for earlier customers' behavior. “Please provide the final image files before layout begins” communicates a dependency when that dependency is established. Preserve deliberate firmness where the situation requires it.

History and founding dates can be primary when they answer a relevant identity or experience question; otherwise place them after the useful description. Keep material suitability restrictions where readers make the affected choice. Customers and applicants may share a page with labeled contact paths; a single audience is not compulsory.

## First-contact drafts

Use a truthful reason for contacting this recipient, a concrete proposed contribution, and a next step proportionate to the relationship and likely value. A known encounter or public request establishes context, not an obligation to respond. Preserve uncertainty about needs and internal process.

Hypothetical supplied facts: a public posting requests help documenting onboarding, the writer authors setup guides, and the writer has a relevant sample they can share. A draft can say: “Your posting asks for help documenting onboarding. I write setup guides and can share a relevant sample. If the role is still open, which application route should I use?” It should not assert that the recipient is losing users or has agreed to a meeting. Without the supplied facts, do not invent this context during a faithful rewrite.

A small existing or requested sample can make a proposal inspectable. A first message need not contain a full plan built on unknown needs. Do not fabricate familiarity or disguise a sales purpose as accidental discovery. An intentionally warm, informal, or distinctive voice can remain truthful. Personalization alone does not establish that a reply will follow.

## Press releases

Identify the actual event and its relevance to the intended recipient. An energetic headline cannot supply a missing event. If relevance is weak, make the factual draft useful and identify that limit; do not manufacture significance or silently refuse the requested writing.

The lead should establish what happened or will happen and why the target reader might care. An invitation needs verified participation details. Organize the body into coherent units that can be reused: event, program, concrete contributions, relevant background, and practical contacts where available. “Partner of the program” and “provided the venue” are different claims; state the actual contribution when it matters.

Use a quote when it adds attributable substance beyond nearby prose: a decision, explanation, or perspective. Retain a supplied ceremonial quote if required; otherwise omission or faithful paraphrase may be more useful. Mark a proposed quote for approval as proposed, never as already spoken. Give descriptive links and the real route for participation or press questions. No structure guarantees coverage.

## Product pages

Start from the reader's situation and decision: what they want to do, what determines fit, and what concern needs evidence. For each main argument, connect a supported property to that situation and select material that lets the reader inspect the connection. Then make the heading, explanation, and caption agree with that evidence.

| Function | Reader question |
|---|---|
| Introduction | What is offered, and is it in the category I need? |
| Argument | How does it fit my situation, and what supports that claim? |
| Detail | What conditions or specifications affect suitability? |
| Transaction | What is the actual offer and requested exchange? |

These are functions, not a mandatory screen order. Price can be useful early. A specification can be the primary argument when it determines compatibility. Do not bury material conditions in a later FAQ to preserve a persuasive sequence.

Hypothetical supplied property: an adjustable document holder supports sheets up to A3. A photograph with an actual A3 sheet can help show fit and orientation. A closeup of its polished hinge does not demonstrate that fit; a staged desk scene does not establish reduced neck strain. A service can be shown through a real output, a process diagram, or a screenshot whose use is authorized when photography would not explain the deliverable.

Keep important specifications and short answers available where readers need them; a downloadable file can supplement them. An expansion control can serve a lengthy secondary answer. Interface implementation, accessibility, and asset use remain part of the relevant product or technical workflow; this reference specifies the information need.

Use customer accounts for concrete expectations, use, outcomes, limitations, and suitability. Preserve informative downsides and attribution. Do not turn an imagined scenario into a testimonial. A compelling review is neither proof of typical results nor permission to publish it. Warranty duration is not established service life.

Personal, narrative, and direct explanatory styles can all fit. Images, icons, columns, and text blocks should perform a useful job; none is categorically required or forbidden. Use relevant observations to assess later revisions without claiming that writing alone caused sales, trust, or retention outcomes.
