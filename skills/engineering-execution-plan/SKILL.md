# Engineering Execution Plan

Use this artifact when a reader needs an actionable plan to deliver an engineering outcome. Do not produce a task inventory when a plan is not useful; use a design document, progress report, or decision memo when that is the actual need.

## Default structure

Adapt the plan to the work:

1. Intended outcome, scope, and non-goals.
2. Constraints, assumptions, and dependencies.
3. Work streams or milestones in delivery order.
4. Deliverables, owners, interfaces, and handoffs when known.
5. Acceptance criteria, validation, rollout, and operational readiness.
6. Risks, mitigations, fallback, and decision points.
7. Immediate next actions and unresolved questions.

## Planning discipline

Sequence work by dependency and risk reduction, not merely by organizational preference. Make validation observable: name the success signal, test, review, or operational check that closes each material milestone. Separate confirmed commitments from proposals and unknowns. Avoid invented estimates, owners, or dates; identify them as planning inputs when they are missing.
