# Decision Memo

Use this artifact when a written brief must enable or request a decision. It is not a general status report; use `progress-reporting` when the primary outcome is an update.

## Default structure

Use the sections needed for the decision:

1. Decision requested or conclusion.
2. Why it matters and the consequence of delay or inaction.
3. Constraints and decision criteria.
4. Evidence and realistic alternatives.
5. Recommendation and trade-offs.
6. Material risks, uncertainties, and mitigations.
7. Requested approval, owner, or next action.

## Memo discipline

Lead with the decision or recommendation. Use numbers, time, cost, scale, and concrete consequences where available. Include the minimum background and technical evidence needed to justify the choice; do not make the reader reconstruct the decision from history. State what would change the recommendation when that condition is material.
