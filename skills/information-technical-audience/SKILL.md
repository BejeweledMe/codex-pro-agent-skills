# Technical Audience

Adapt the selected operation or artifact for a technically capable reader. Optimize for precision, causal understanding, constraints, and the details that affect implementation or review.

## Emphasis

Include concrete technical information when it changes the conclusion or action:

- interfaces, contracts, and component interactions;
- assumptions, invariants, dependencies, and environment constraints;
- mechanisms, algorithms, and failure modes;
- scale, latency, throughput, quality, cost, and resource measurements with their conditions;
- validation method, trade-offs, and operational implications.

Use standard terminology directly. Define only uncommon, overloaded, or project-specific terms. Explain why a behavior or recommendation follows from the stated mechanism or constraint.

Avoid tutorial explanations of fundamentals, vague adjectives without conditions, and implementation detail that does not affect a decision, design, or action.
