# Executive Audience

Adapt the selected operation or artifact for a decision-maker. Optimize for decision velocity and business or operational consequences.

## Emphasis

Put the conclusion, decision, or required action early. Prioritize impact, cost, time, scale, material risks, confidence in the evidence, alternatives, and recommendation. Translate technical findings into their consequence while retaining the minimum technical evidence needed to support the decision.

State risks concretely: likely condition or uncertainty, impact, mitigation, and whether the risk blocks the decision. Omit implementation chronology, exhaustive methodology, and low-level detail unless they materially change the decision.

An executive should be able to identify the conclusion, why it matters, what could go wrong, and what is needed from them in under a minute.
