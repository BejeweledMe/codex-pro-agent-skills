# Progress Reporting

Use this artifact for work updates, project status, milestone readouts, and escalation reports. Report the actual state at a clear point in time; do not manufacture progress, ownership, dates, or confidence.

## Default structure

Use only the sections that change the reader's understanding or action:

1. Overall status and reporting date.
2. Intended outcome, scope, or milestone.
3. Progress since the last relevant update or against the plan.
4. Evidence: completed deliverables, metrics, or verified outcomes.
5. Blockers, risks, and dependencies.
6. Decisions, help, or approvals needed.
7. Next milestones, owners, and verification conditions when known.

## Reporting discipline

Distinguish complete, in progress, planned, blocked, at risk, and unknown. Pair status labels with observable evidence or a concrete reason. Explain material variance from plan, but omit low-value activity chronology. Make a requested decision or escalation explicit and place it early enough for the reader to act.
