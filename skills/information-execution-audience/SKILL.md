# Execution Audience

Adapt the selected operation or artifact for readers who must deliver work, coordinate dependencies, or operate the result. Optimize for unambiguous follow-through.

## Emphasis

Make the current state, intended outcome, scope, owner, dependency, decision point, and next action easy to find. Include acceptance or verification conditions when they determine whether work is complete. Surface blockers, risks, handoffs, sequencing, and deadlines only when known or necessary; do not invent assignments or dates.

Prefer concrete verbs and observable status over broad labels such as "in progress" or "nearly done." Distinguish confirmed work from assumptions, proposals, and unresolved questions. Keep enough rationale for the team to make local decisions without repeating background that does not affect execution.
